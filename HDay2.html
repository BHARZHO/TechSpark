Part 7 • Box Sizing

✨ The Magic of `box-sizing: border-box`

Normally, adding padding or border expands the total width of your box. border-box keeps the width strictly at declared size!

* { box-sizing: border-box; } .box { width: 300px; padding: 30px; border: 5px solid black; }

Total Rendered Width: 300px

Part 9 • Quick Quiz

🧠 Advanced Quiz: Test Your Might!

Click any card to reveal the answer:

1. What is padding?
Space INSIDE the box (pushes content away from border).
2. What is margin?
Space OUTSIDE the box (pushes element away from neighbors).
3. What does TRBL stand for?
Top, Right, Bottom, Left (directional shorthand order).
4. What 3 properties define a border?
Thickness (e.g. 2px), Style (solid/dashed/dotted), Color (black).
5. What does `box-sizing: border-box` do?
Makes declared width/height include padding and border.
6. What does `padding: 20px 30px;` mean?
Top & Bottom = 20px, Left & Right = 30px.
7. Does margin increase the space inside the box?
No! Margin creates space OUTSIDE the box.
8. What is the innermost layer of the box model?
The Content layer.
