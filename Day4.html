                    Part 10 • Quick Quiz
                    
🧠 Classroom Quiz: Test Your Might!

                


                
Students, raise your hands! Click any card to reveal the secret answer:


                

                    

                        
1. What does the <br> tag do?

                        
It pushes text to a new line (breaks line).

                    


                    

                        
2. Does <hr> need a closing tag?

                        
No! It is a self-closing tag that draws a horizontal line.

                    


                    

                        
3. How do you make text bold?

                        
Use the <strong> tag.

                    


                    

                        
4. Which heading is the largest?

                        
<h1> is the largest heading tag.

                    


                    

                        
5. What tag builds links?

                        
The anchor tag <a> with the "href" attribute.

                    


                    

                        
6. What does "alt" mean in <img>?

                        
Alternative text: it describes the photo if it fails to load.

                    


                    

                        
7. Does <img> have a closing tag?

                        
No, <img> is a self-closing tag!

                    


                    

                        
8. Where does a link open?

                        
In the same browser tab, unless customized.
